Exam Guidelines • CAPS Paper 2

This is the study guide Mpumi can follow. It explains the sections, how to study them, and gives high‑yield tips to score marks. Use the blue links to jump straight to matching practice questions.

1) How the Paper is Structured

  • Time: 3 hours
  • Marks: 150
  • Layout: Q1 MCQ (10×2=20) + Q2–Q9 structured on key topics
  • Skills tested: basic recall, explain/understand, apply & analyse, evaluate. Expect graphs, calculations and short explanations.

2) Study Advice to Get High Marks

A. Easy marks first
  • Learn short definitions (activation energy, conjugate base, boiling point, oxidation).
  • Practice trend questions (BP vs chain length, rate vs temperature) — write the key reason using the right words.
  • Show formulas and substitutions. Units every step. Round at the end.
B. Core calculations
  • Stoichiometry: moles, limiting reagent, % yield, % purity.
  • Gas molar volume at STP (22.4 dm³·mol⁻¹).
  • ΔH using bond enthalpies.
  • Kc with ICE tables.
  • Titrations and pH around equivalence.
  • E°cell = E°(cath) − E°(an).
C. Organics = big section
  • IUPAC naming up to 8 carbons.
  • Isomers: chain, position, functional.
  • Reactions: addition, substitution, elimination, esterification (conditions!).
D. Exam routine
  • MCQs first for momentum.
  • Then 1 structured question per topic.
  • Finally full timed papers (3 h). Mark your work and correct errors.
